# 0.1.2.0

- The plugin checks in a few seconds after the mount connects, disconnects,
  parks, unparks or slews. The mount's state and position reach the other
  rigs right away, not at the next 5-minute check-in.
- With no mount connected, the telescope reports `idle` with no position.
  `offline` is sent only in the last check-in, when N.I.N.A. closes.
- After a failed check-in, the plugin retries within 10 minutes at most. A
  short server outage no longer leaves the telescope past the server's
  25-minute online window.
